Common topics

Restoring a Premium purchase

If Premium isn't showing up after reinstalling the app or switching devices, email us with the platform (iOS or Android) and the email or account used for the original purchase.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what puzzle type you were playing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps if you have one.

Requesting a puzzle type

We're actively adding new 60-second puzzle formats. Send your idea — every puzzle still has to fit in a genuine 60 seconds, free or Premium.

Account or data questions

The app doesn't require an account and stores almost nothing about you locally on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture, or just ask.
