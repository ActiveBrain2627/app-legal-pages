60 Second Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 4, 2026

This policy explains what information 60 Second Puzzles collects, why, and what choices you have. We built this app to work with as little data as possible — most of what makes the app function stays on your device.

1Who this app is for

60 Second Puzzles is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us at sixtysecondpuzzles.app@proton.me and we will delete it.

2Information we collect directly

60 Second Puzzles does not require an account, login, or sign-up to use. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any personal identifier to play.

Your scores, streaks, difficulty progress, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. We do not receive, see, or store this data on any server we control. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

3Information collected by third-party services

To keep the free version of the app running, we use third-party services that may collect limited technical information as described below. We do not control these services' data practices directly — each operates under its own privacy policy, linked below.

  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID), general device information, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status for the paid version of the app across the App Store and Google Play. RevenueCat receives purchase/transaction data from Apple or Google to confirm your subscription — it does not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — process all payments directly. We never see or store your payment card information.

4Your choices

  • Ad personalization — you can limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reduce how AdMob personalizes ads shown to you.
  • Subscription management — manage or cancel a subscription any time through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not through us directly.
  • Deleting your data — since gameplay data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account for us to delete.

5Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information. Because we hold no personal account data ourselves, most such requests will be minimal to fulfill, but you can still reach us at sixtysecondpuzzles.app@proton.me with any request, and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

6Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data associated with the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Data processed by our third-party providers (AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) is subject to their own security practices.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes — for example, when we add new features or third-party services. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at sixtysecondpuzzles.app@proton.me.

Note: This policy was drafted to accurately describe how 60 Second Puzzles actually works. It is a starting point, not a substitute for review by a qualified privacy attorney, particularly before wide release across multiple countries with different privacy laws (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California).
