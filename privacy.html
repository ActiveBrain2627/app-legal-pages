60 Second Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy explains what information 60 Second Puzzles collects, why, and what choices you have. We built this app to work with as little data as possible — most of what makes the app function stays on your device.

1Who this app is for

60 Second Puzzles is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and the ads shown in the app are not designed for children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us at feedback.tribune012@passinbox.com and we will delete it.

2Information we collect directly

60 Second Puzzles does not require an account, login, or sign-up to use. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any personal identifier to play.

The app stores a small amount of information only on your device, using standard on-device storage: your best score; how many rounds you have played today and how many bonus rounds you have earned (for the daily limits); a count of your correct answers for each puzzle type (used to unlock new puzzle variations); whether Premium is active; and details of your current subscription plan (which plan, when it renews or expires, whether it will renew, and any pending plan switch). This information is not linked to your name or identity, and we do not receive, see, or store it on any server we control. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Your device's own backup features (such as iCloud or Google backup) may back up app data under your device settings; we do not control those.

As of the date above, the app does not include any analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. If we add any, we will update this policy.

3Information collected by third-party services

To keep the free version of the app running and to handle subscriptions, the app includes third-party services that may collect limited technical information as described below. We do not control these services' data practices — each operates under its own privacy policy, linked below.

  • Google AdMob — shows banner, full-screen, and rewarded video ads in the free version of the app. AdMob (Google) may collect and use your IP address, device and advertising identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), general device information, and information about how you interact with ads, to serve, measure, and personalize ads where permitted and to prevent fraud. Google's consent tool asks for your permission where required, and on iOS the app shows Apple's tracking-permission prompt. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.
  • RevenueCat — confirms your subscription status on our behalf across the App Store and Google Play. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID that it generates, purchase and transaction data from Apple or Google, and basic technical information (such as your device and app version and IP address). We do not give RevenueCat your name, email address, or any other identifier, and it does not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — process all payments directly and handle app distribution under their own policies. We never see or store your payment card information.

4Legal basis for processing (EEA/UK users)

We do not currently offer the app to the public in the member states of the European Union. If you are in the UK or another European country where the app is available, the legal bases for the limited processing involved are: your consent (for personalized advertising, collected through Google's consent tool where required — you may withdraw it at any time; see Section 6); performance of a contract (confirming your subscription so you get what you paid for); and our legitimate interests (operating the app and preventing fraud).

5Data retention

We do not operate servers that store your personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on request; if a request concerns data held by a third party, we will point you to that provider. Gameplay data lives only on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data held by AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google is retained under each provider's own retention policy, linked in Section 3.

6Your choices

  • Ad personalization — on iOS, you can decline Apple's tracking prompt or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (typically Settings → Privacy → Ads; exact steps vary by device). Where Google's consent form is shown, choose the options you prefer. The app does not currently include its own button for reopening that form.
  • Subscription management — manage or cancel a subscription any time through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not through us directly. The "Manage or cancel subscription" link on the app's "Your plan" screen opens that page.
  • Deleting your data — since the data described in Section 2 lives only on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. There is no server-side account for us to delete. Data held by Google, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google Play is governed by their policies.

7Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access the personal information associated with you; correct inaccurate information; request deletion; object to or restrict certain processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time (for processing based on consent, such as personalized ads). Because we hold no personal account data ourselves, most such requests will be minimal for us to fulfill directly, but you can still reach us at feedback.tribune012@passinbox.com with any request, and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. For data held by Google, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google Play, you will need to contact that provider. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell your personal information for money. Our ad provider (AdMob) may use advertising identifiers to show and measure ads, which some laws treat as "sharing" or "sale." The app does not currently offer its own opt-out control for this; you can limit it with the device controls described in Section 6. You may also email us and we will respond, although we cannot change what third parties collect on their own.

8International data transfers

We're based in the United States, and our third-party providers (AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these providers rely on legal safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers — see each provider's own privacy policy (Section 3) for details.

9Data security

We do not operate servers that receive your personal data from the app. The small amount of data the app stores on your device relies on your device's own security features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Data processed by our third-party providers (AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) is subject to their own security practices.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes — for example, when we add new features or third-party services. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. If a change is material, we'll make reasonable efforts to give you more notice (for example, in an app update's release notes or on this page). Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at feedback.tribune012@passinbox.com.
